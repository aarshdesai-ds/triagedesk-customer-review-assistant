# Warranty

## Coverage
All electronics include a warranty covering hardware faults. The warranty length depends on the product and is listed in the product catalog.

## Not covered
Water damage, drops, and damage caused by third-party chargers are not covered.

## Claims
Warranty claims result in a repair or a replacement, never a cash refund. Claims are processed within 7 business days of receiving the item.

## Extended warranty
One extra year of coverage can be bought within 30 days of purchase for 10% of the product price.
