# Troubleshooting

## Bluetooth connection drops
For earbuds or headphones that keep disconnecting, hold both buttons for 10 seconds to reset them, then re-pair with your device. If the drops continue after a reset, file a warranty claim.

## Blender smells of burning
A slight smell during the first one or two uses is normal. A strong burning smell after that means the motor may be faulty: stop using the blender and raise a return or warranty claim.

## Device not charging
Use the original cable and a charger rated at least 10W. Damage from third-party chargers is not covered by warranty.
