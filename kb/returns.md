# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened headphones and earbuds can only be returned within 14 days of delivery, for hygiene reasons.

## Condition
Items must include all original accessories and packaging. Items with physical damage caused by the customer are not eligible for return.

## Damaged or defective items
Report a damaged or defective item within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. These items get a free replacement if stock is available, and a full refund otherwise.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Refunds as store credit are instant.

## Return shipping
Return pickup is free for damaged or defective items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat Rs. 99 fee is deducted from the refund.
