# Kestrel Rewards

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point for every Rs. 100 spent, and 50 bonus points for each product review.

## Redeeming points
100 points are worth Rs. 50 off. Points can cover at most 20% of an order's value.

## Expiry and tiers
Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Gold members, who spend more than Rs. 50,000 in a calendar year, get free express delivery.
