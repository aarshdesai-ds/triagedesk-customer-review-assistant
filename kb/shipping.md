# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business days elsewhere. Express delivery is available in metro cities only and arrives the next business day for orders placed before 2 PM.

## Delivery costs
Standard delivery is free on orders above Rs. 999; otherwise it costs Rs. 49. Express delivery costs Rs. 149.

## Late deliveries
Orders not delivered within 10 business days of dispatch qualify for a refund of the delivery fee. Customers can also cancel a late order for a full refund.

## Tracking
Customers can track orders from the My Orders page. We only deliver within India.
